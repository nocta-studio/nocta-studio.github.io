Privacy policy - Nocta Backup

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Nocta Backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It requests access to Google Drive (to store encrypted backups and read files the owner places in a designated folder), Gmail (read-only) and Google Calendar (read-only).

Data accessed through Google APIs is processed only on servers controlled by the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Backups are encrypted before upload. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
